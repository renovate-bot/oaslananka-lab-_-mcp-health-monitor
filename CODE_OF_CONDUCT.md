# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Participants are expected to keep project spaces professional, respectful, and
focused on the health of the software and its users.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and precise language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Keep security reports private until coordinated disclosure is complete.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Disruptive conduct that prevents maintainers or users from collaborating.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the maintainer through the repository security
advisory channel or the contact listed in package metadata. Maintainers may
remove comments, close issues, or block participation when needed to protect the
project.
